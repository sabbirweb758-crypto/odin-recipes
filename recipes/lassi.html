Ingredients for making lassi

  • Yogurt (Dahi): Plain, thick yogurt is the star of the drink.
  • Chilled Water or Milk: Used to thin out the yogurt to your preferred consistency.
  • Ice Cubes: To make the drink ice-cold and refreshing.
  • Sugar: Or any sweetener like honey or maple syrup.Flavorings: A few drops of rose water, cardamom powder (elaichi), or saffron (kesar).

🍯 How to Make Sweet Lassi

  1. Add Base: Pour 1 cup of thick yogurt and 1/2 cup of chilled water (or milk) into a blender.
  2. Sweeten: Add 4 to 5 tablespoons of sugar, according to your taste.
  3. Flavor: Toss in a pinch of cardamom powder or a few drops of rose water for a beautiful aroma.
  4. Blend: Mix everything on high speed for 1 to 2 minutes until the sugar dissolves and a frothy layer forms on top.
  5. Serve: Pour into a glass over ice cubes and enjoy cold
