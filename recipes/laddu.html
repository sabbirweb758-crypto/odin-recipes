Laddu page

Discription

uniquely prepared by first frying or baking dough portions, breaking them down into a coarse powder, and then rolling them into spheres. When you cut or bite into it, the texture beautifully collapses into a rich, grainy blend of roasted flour, aromatic ghee, and sweet sugar or jaggery. Heavily infused with cardamom and packed with finely chopped nuts, it offers a deeply satisfying, melt-in-the-mouth texture that is both crunchy and incredibly soft at the same time."

Ingredients for making laddu

🌾 The Flour Base (For the Dough)
  • Coarse Wheat Flour (Atta): 2 cups (Provides the main rustic texture).
  • Semolina (Suji/Rava): 4 tablespoons (Makes it perfectly grainy and crispy).
  • Gram Flour (Besan): 1/4 cup (Adds a deep, nutty flavor).
  • • Warm Water or Milk: As needed to bind a very tight, firm dough

How to make laddu

  1. 🥣 Step 1: Make the Dough• Mix Flours: Combine 2 cups coarse wheat flour, 4 tbsp suji, 1/4 cup besan, and 4 tbsp ghee in a mixing bowl. Rub Ghee: Rub the mixture together with your fingertips until it feels like coarse breadcrumbs. Knead Firmly: Add warm water or milk in small splashes to form a very tight, hard dough.
  2. 🟤 Step 2: Shape & Fry the Muthia• Shape Dough: Divide the dough and press them in your fist to make rough, elongated fist-shaped balls called muthias.• Heat Ghee: Heat plenty of ghee (or oil) in a deep pan on medium-low heat.• Deep Fry: Fry the muthias very slowly until they turn deep golden brown and crispy all the way to the inside (takes about 10–12 minutes).
  3. Step 3: Cool & Crush • Cool Down: Remove the fried muthias from the ghee and let them cool down completely. • Break Pieces: Break the cooled muthias into smaller, rough chunks using your hands. • Grind Coarsely: Place the chunks into a blender or food processor and pulse them into a coarse, grainy powder.
  4. 🍯 Step 4: Sweeten & Add Flavors • Melt Sweetener: Melt 1.5 cups of chopped jaggery (or sugar) with 2 tbsp ghee in a small pan over low heat until it liquefies. Do not overcook or boil it. • Combine Ingredients: Pour the melted jaggery over your crushed powder. • Add Aromatics: Toss in 1/2 tsp cardamom powder, a pinch of nutmeg powder, and 1/4 cup chopped nuts. Mix everything thoroughly with a spoon.
  5. ☄️ Step 5: Roll into Laddus • Add Warm Ghee: Pour 1/2 cup of hot, melted ghee into the mixture to give it moisture for binding. • Shape Spheres: Take small portions of the warm mixture in your hands and press tightly to roll them into neat, round balls. • Finish: Roll the shaped laddus in poppy seeds (khus khus) to coat the outside. Let them set completely before serving.
